FUNKY Support

See what's happening tonight, right around you.

Contact us

Questions, problems, or something to report? Email seanauble@icloud.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a day.

Report a post, story or person

In the app: tap the flag on a story, the ••• on a post, or ••• on someone's profile and choose Report. Every report is reviewed and objectionable content is removed within 24 hours.

Block someone

Open their profile, tap ••• and choose Block. They can't message you, friend you or comment on your posts. Unblock any time in Settings → Blocked accounts.

Delete your account

Settings → Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile and everything you posted.

Location

FUNKY uses your location to show what's happening in the area you choose. Your exact location is never shown to anyone, and posts only show a location if you tag a place. You can turn location off in your iPhone's Settings.

Who can use FUNKY

FUNKY is for people 21 and older.
