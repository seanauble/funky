FUNKY payment card logos
========================

Drop the official logo files here with these exact names, then push.
The payment cards, the "Request money" sheet and Settings > Payment apps
pick them up automatically. Until a file is here, that app's button just
shows its color and name (nothing breaks).

  venmo.png      Venmo
  cashapp.png    Cash App
  paypal.png     PayPal
  zelle.png      Zelle

Format: a square PNG of the app's icon/mark, at least 128 x 128 px.
They show on a small white tile, so the full-color square icon looks best.

Get each one from the company's own brand / press page (search
"Venmo brand guidelines", "Cash App press kit", "PayPal logo center",
"Zelle brand guidelines") and follow their rules for using it.

Apple Cash: leave this one out. Apple only allows the Apple Pay mark on
real Apple Pay checkouts, and FUNKY's Apple Cash button just opens
Messages, so it stays a plain "Apple Cash" button. (applecash.png is
supported if Apple ever gives you an approved badge for it.)
